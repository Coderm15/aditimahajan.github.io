BoloBit · Founder

Building BoloBit: AI Surveys That Find the Customers Who Are Quietly Leaving

0→1 AI Feedback Intelligence Platform | 2025–Present

The Challenge

Satisfaction is not loyalty. Most survey tools measure only how customers feel, so the accounts that are content but using the product less and wavering on renewal never show up until they churn. Designing a good survey, launching it and turning the answers into action also takes days of manual work.

My Approach

Describe it: A business describes what it needs to learn in one sentence; BoloBit drafts the survey with AI and tags every question with the signal it measures: Sentiment, Behaviour or Intent.

Launch it: Shared by link, QR code, short code or email; respondents answer on any device with no account.

Act on it: When the survey closes, each answer is verified against what its question was designed to measure, the three signals are crossed per customer, and the report names who is at risk, why, and the next step for each account.
