Satisfaction is not loyalty.

The easiest way to design and launch a customer survey, with the analysis and next steps ready the moment it closes.

Most surveys measure how customers feel. The customers you lose are often the ones who feel fine, use the product less each month, and quietly decide not to renew. BoloBit measures three signals per customer and crosses them, so those accounts show up by name before the renewal date.

The question this survey was run to answer

Identify which customers are at risk of not renewing, and why, so retention effort can be targeted before renewal dates.

The answer

14 of 34 respondents (41%) are at risk of not renewing. Of these, 9 are not unhappy: they are disengaging. A sentiment-only tool would count them as satisfied and miss them.

When a survey closes, BoloBit checks whether its results are solid enough to learn from. If they are, "Create similar survey" designs the next one using what this one proved, and what it got wrong.
